**Askwell is unsigned.** macOS and Windows will warn on first launch — that is expected, not
a defect. Read **[Installing Askwell](https://github.com/Rumeasiyan/askwell/blob/main/docs/installing.md)**
before you do anything else: it explains the warning, how to get past it, and — the part that
actually matters — how to verify the download against the `SHA256SUMS` published below before
you run anything.

**Verify the checksum first.** That is the check that protects you; the warning-bypass steps
only stop your computer asking.
